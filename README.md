# 🌸 Ekaa – Personal Pocket Mentor  
*Empower yourself, one minute at a time.*

Ekaa is a mobile-first empowerment platform designed to uplift women, girls, homemakers, and underconfident learners by delivering bite-sized, real-life education — using AI, audio, and community-powered learning.

> ✨ Built for the Maverick Effect AI Challenge 2025 – Digital Literacy & Skill Empowerment

---

## 🧭 What is Ekaa?

Ekaa is not just an app — it's a **movement**.  
It enables personal growth through:

- 1-minute audio lessons  
- Real-world roleplay simulations  
- Voice journaling with AI feedback  
- Anonymous, women-only Q&A community  

Whether you're preparing for your first job, visiting a clinic, or simply building confidence — **Ekaa is your everyday mentor in your pocket**.

---

## 🎯 Vision & Mission

> "Empower every woman with the confidence to face the real world — one minute at a time."

Our mission is to democratize access to **practical life knowledge** for underserved women using AI, local languages, and mobile-first design.

---

## 💡 Core Features

### 🎧 Daily Dose
- 1-minute audio lessons on:
  - Spoken English
  - Hygiene, health, and well-being
  - Digital awareness and safety
  - Financial basics
  - Women’s rights and safety

### 🎭 Roleplay Simulations
- Practice real-life interactions:
  - Talking at a bank or clinic
  - Reporting to police
  - Attending job interviews
  - Speaking on stage

### 🎤 AI Confidence Journal
- Record your voice daily
- Get AI feedback on:
  - Tone, fluency, and clarity
  - Growth tracking
  - Suggested speaking tips

### 🤝 Teach Me Sister – Anonymous Community
- Women-to-women Q&A space
- Ask about life, parenting, money, safety, emotions
- Supportive answers from peers and mentors
